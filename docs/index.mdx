# Teleoperating the arm

Operator input for the Kinova Gen3: an **Xbox pad** and a **Meta Quest 3**
controller, each a ROS 2 node that drives the arm through
[`kinova-gen3-ros2`](/kinova-gen3-ros2)'s streaming tier.

Both nodes do the same thing at the arm end. They acquire control, open a stream
on one controller, and publish a target every tick for as long as they run.
Nothing moves until the operator engages: a stick deflection on the pad, a grip
squeeze on the Quest. Let go and the arm holds where it is.

| Input    | Controller                      | What the target is                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Xbox pad | `ee_twist` (stiff)              | the stick deflection, streamed as a base-frame twist            |
| Quest 3  | `ee_pose_impedance` (compliant) | the controller's motion since the squeeze, applied to the EE pose, inside a workspace box and per-tick step caps |

## What it gives you

- **Two inputs, one contract.** A shared session base handles the arm side
  (acquire, open, stream, close, recover) so the input nodes only turn
  their device into a target.
- **The Quest plugged straight into the Jetson.** The headset talks to the
  robot over a USB-C cable and adb. No laptop in the loop.
- **Calibration from the controller.** The Quest's tracking yaw is arbitrary
  per headset boot. Hold A, move your hand forward once, and the alignment is
  fixed and saved.
- **A headset that stays awake.** The reader defeats the proximity sensor on
  connect and relaunches the headset app if it dies, so an unworn headset on
  the bench keeps tracking.

## Two things that surprise people

**One input at a time.** The arm has one owner token and one stream, and the
driver's `/acquire_control` seizes. Starting a second teleop node halts the
first. Pick one alternative in your sheppy profile.

**The Quest sleeps its controller, not just itself.** With hand tracking's
auto-switch on, the headset puts an idle controller to sleep. Its buttons keep
working over Bluetooth while its pose freezes, which looks exactly like a bug in
the reader. The fix is a headset setting, described in
[Setting up the Quest](/rammp-teleop/quest-setup#turn-off-controller-auto-sleep).
